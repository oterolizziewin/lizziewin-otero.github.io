How the spreadsheet works

  • Status and Priority use dropdown lists, so entries stay consistent.
  • A Days left column counts down to the due date with a formula, and turns red when a task is past due and not done.
  • A Summary sheet counts tasks by status and owner with COUNTIF and COUNTIFS, so the totals update by themselves.
  • The header row is frozen and filters are on, so the list can be sorted by owner or due date.
