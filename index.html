A About

About me

I graduated Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Arts in English Language Studies (Business Process Outsourcing track) from Mindanao State University–General Santos. The BPO track trained me in business communication and customer-facing writing; the English program trained me to read closely, write precisely, and edit other people's work.

My undergraduate thesis, a grammar sketch of Maguindanaon-Laya, received the Best Thesis Award. Documentation research depends on keeping recordings, transcriptions, notes, and drafts organized and traceable, which is the same discipline good records management needs. I have since co-authored a paper on the language's verbal morphology that was accepted at PACLIC 40, an international linguistics conference.

Alongside my studies, I worked on the creative and public information side of student organizations and as a publication layout artist, producing magazines, posters, infographics, presentations, and social media materials.

I'm now looking for an administrative assistant role where I can put these habits to daily use: preparing documents, managing schedules and correspondence, keeping records organized, and helping a team communicate clearly.

How I run a page

  1. Plan the postsDecide what to post and when: teasers and announcements before an event, product features and behind-the-scenes content for the business.
  2. Design the pubmatsCreate every graphic in Canva and Photoshop, keeping each page's colors, logo, and style consistent.
  3. Write the captionsWrite clear captions with the key details, in English and the local language where it fits the audience.
  4. PublishPost the materials and keep the cover photo, profile, and page information up to date.
  5. EngageCheck comments and messages and answer questions from followers and customers.
  6. Follow upPost recognition, thank-you, and behind-the-scenes content to keep the page active between big announcements.
