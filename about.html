About

Hi, I'm Lizziewin.

I'm a designer who writes and a writer who organizes. That mix happens to be what most busy people need help with.

I graduated Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Arts in English Language Studies from Mindanao State University–General Santos, on the Business Process Outsourcing track. My thesis won Best Thesis Award, and I've presented research at four international conferences.

Since 2023 I've worked as a freelance graphic designer and academic editor. I design social media graphics, posters, magazines, books, and presentations, and I edit manuscripts until they're clear and ready to publish. I also run two Facebook pages from start to finish, from the first pubmat to the last reply.

My research background shows in how I work. I keep files organized, check details twice, write things down, and meet deadlines. During my internship at the LTFRB, that meant translating official materials into three languages, running field surveys, and preparing technical reports.

If you need someone who can make the graphic, write the caption, fix the document, and keep track of all of it, that's where I'm most useful.
