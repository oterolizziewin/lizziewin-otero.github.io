Professional Email Correspondence

Four common situations. Each email states its purpose in the first line and ends with a clear next step.

1 · Confirming a meeting

FromAdmin Assistant <admin@brightwater.example>ToMs. Carla Gomez <carla.gomez@mail.example>SubjectConfirming: Learner progress meeting, Tue 20 Oct, 3:00 PM

Dear Ms. Gomez,

This is to confirm your meeting with Ms. Ana Reyes, our Center Director, on Tuesday, 20 October, at 3:00 PM at the Brightwater Learning Center, Room 2.

The meeting will take about 30 minutes. Ms. Reyes will go over Daniel's reading progress this term and the options for the next level. If you have his latest report card, please bring it along.

Could you reply to confirm by Monday, 19 October? If the time no longer works, I'm happy to suggest another slot.

Kind regards,
Admin Assistant
Brightwater Community Learning Center

2 · Following up on a missing document

FromAdmin Assistant <admin@brightwater.example>ToMr. Leo Tan <leo.tan@mail.example>SubjectFollow-up: signed volunteer agreement needed by Fri 23 Oct

Dear Mr. Tan,

Thank you again for volunteering as a Saturday tutor. I'm following up on my 9 October email about the volunteer agreement. We haven't received your signed copy yet.

We need it on file before your first session on 31 October. You can sign the attached PDF and reply to this email with a scan or photo, or drop off a printed copy at the front desk any weekday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

If you have any questions about the agreement, just let me know.

Best regards,
Admin Assistant
