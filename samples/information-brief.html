Booking Appointments: Three Options for the Front Desk

To
Ana Reyes, Center Director
From
Admin Assistant
Date
15 October 2026
Purpose
Help decide how parents and visitors should book appointments

Summary

Appointments are now booked by phone and written in a paper logbook. This causes double bookings and missed reminders, and only the person at the desk can see the schedule. I looked at three options. I recommend Option B, a shared online calendar managed by the front desk, as a low-cost first step that we can set up within a week.

The problem

  • Staff cannot check the Director's availability when they are away from the desk.
  • There is no automatic reminder, so visitors sometimes forget appointments.
  • The logbook has no backup if it is lost or damaged.

Options compared

A. Keep the paper logbookB. Shared online calendarC. Self-service booking page
How it worksDesk writes every booking by handDesk enters bookings in a shared calendar that staff can viewVisitors pick an open slot online; calendar updates itself
CostNoneNone with the office's existing Google or Microsoft accountMay need a paid plan, depending on the tool
Setup timeNoneAbout 1 week, including a short staff briefing2–3 weeks, plus testing
RemindersNoneEmail reminders can be set on each eventUsually automatic
Main riskDouble bookings, no backupDepends on desk keeping it updatedVisitors without internet access are left out; needs ongoing management

Recommendation

Start with Option B. It solves the visibility and backup problems at no extra cost, and staff already use the same accounts. After three months, we can review whether visitors would benefit from booking online themselves (Option C).

Next steps if approved

  1. Create the shared calendar and set viewing rights for staff (Admin Assistant, 1 day).
  2. Copy the next 30 days of bookings from the logbook (Admin Assistant, 1 day).
  3. Brief staff for 15 minutes at the November meeting.
  4. Keep the logbook in parallel for two weeks, then retire it.

This brief compares general features and is meant for an internal decision. Specific plans and prices should be checked with the provider before purchase.
