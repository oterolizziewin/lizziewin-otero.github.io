File naming convention

BW-[DEPT]-[YEAR]-[NNN]_[short-title]_[YYYY-MM-DD]

Example: BW-ADM-2026-001_staff-minutes-oct_2026-10-08.pdf

  • Department codes: ADM (Administration), FIN (Finance), PRG (Programs), STU (Student Records), VOL (Volunteers)
  • Dates use year-month-day so files sort in order.
  • Lowercase words joined by hyphens; no spaces, no "final-final-v2".
