1. Approval of September minutes

Approved without changes. Moved by M. Santos, seconded by R. Lim.

2. Q3 enrollment and attendance

M. Santos reported that enrollment rose in Q3, mainly in the evening English class. Attendance dropped in August during the rainy-season class suspensions. A full summary will go to the board.

3. Year-end learners' showcase

The team agreed on Saturday, 12 December as the preferred date. Two venues were discussed: the center's hall (free, seats about 60) and the barangay covered court (larger, needs a permit).

Decision: Hold the showcase on 12 December. J. Dela Cruz will get quotes for chairs and sound equipment for both venues before the final choice at the November meeting.

4. Moving shared files to one cloud drive

Files are currently spread across personal laptops and two USB drives. The Admin Assistant proposed one shared drive with folders by department and year, and a standard file-naming rule. Staff agreed. The Director asked that student records stay in a restricted folder.

Decision: Adopt the shared drive starting 1 November. Student records go in a folder only the Director and program heads can open.

5. November tutor schedule

R. Lim has confirmed eight volunteer tutors for November. Two Saturday slots are still unfilled.
